# October 5, 2026: Started the project and made the schematic + pcb

I got inspired by that one weird instrument you could buy at a music store for 5$, you played it by bending metal bars that made different sounds that annoyed everyone except the person playing it. As my first hack club project (that i plan on completing) i thought it would be a good idea to make the same idea but digital, with the same simplicity. It would consist of a central brain (esp32 supermini i had laying around), 8 piano keys (buttons) and 2 buttons to record/play a melody you made or to switch tones of the keys, a 18650 Li-ion battery with tp4056 charging module for portability and passive buzzer to make the noise. All of this encased in a plastic 3d printed case. I attached photos of the schematic and pcb, i would appreciate if someone checked it and confirmed its good to go because its my first time making a professional pcb and not a 3d printed copper tape one ![](https://fabricate.hackclub-assets.com/9c45ce1c163395cf0401e64bc0dd86bf0819914950448ce61784171578ea2a4f/image.png)
